A few useful things.

Where is the NetOn icon?

Look next to the clock in the Windows taskbar. If it isn't visible, click the small arrow to see the hidden icons.

What happens when I turn it back on?

NetOn restores the connections that were enabled before you switched off. Your PC can then reconnect as usual.

How do I close NetOn?

Right-click its icon and choose “Exit NetOn”. If you exit while your connections are off, open NetOn again and switch them back on when you're ready.
