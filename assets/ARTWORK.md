# NetOn artwork

`neton-illustration.png` was created with the built-in image-generation tool for NetOn's installer and download page. Installer BMPs and the website WebP are resized/cropped encodings of that original. Wizard art is supplied at four times the standard NSIS dimensions for high-DPI screens.

Prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create premium installer artwork for Voltura NetOn, a simple Windows app that turns internet access on and off. A tall portrait illustration, 2:3 aspect ratio, designed to remain beautiful in a narrow vertical crop. Deep forest-green background with subtle grain and soft light. Centered in the middle, a beautifully rendered dimensional physical toggle switch, emerald green in the ON position, ivory circular thumb, a restrained tiny white power glyph on the thumb. Fine curved network connection lines radiate gently around it, with a few small warm ivory nodes. Understated Scandinavian software branding, clean, calm, tactile matte materials, elegant generous negative space above and below. Muted lime highlights, ivory and emerald, no neon cyberpunk. No computer screens, no Windows logo, no extra switches. No words, letters, labels, numbers, captions, or watermarks. High quality 3D editorial illustration, not a UI screenshot. The same artwork will also appear in a website hero.
